# TOPMANSHOPWORLD — Interactive Automotive Digital Showroom

## Positioning
Visitor **explores modernization options**, not a flat SKU grid. Brand stays TOPMANSHOPWORLD (studio dark + warm metal accent).

## Architecture (two layers)
1. **Native uCoz E-commerce** — categories Lighting / Body / Multimedia, product cards, cart, orders (`shop_*`).
2. **Interactive Experience** — `/workspace/showroom/` hero (Three.js stylized vehicle), hotspots, motion. Progressive enhancement; reduced-motion & no-WebGL fallbacks.

## What was / was not published
- **Done on live shop data:** categories `lighting`, `body`, `multimedia` + sample SKUs.
- **Not published:** site-wide template/CSS/framework replace. Showroom HTML is a **local prototype** until the owner approves `templates_write` / custom Page deploy.

## Fitment honesty
3D/CSS vehicle is **stylized**. Compatibility Check form is the commercial gate — never auto-claim OEM fit from visuals.
